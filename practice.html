Your practice

Every practice Psyche has written for you, with the conversation it came from.

The river doesn’t ask you to jump — it just keeps moving, and sooner or later your feet get wet.

The pattern

Every message so far has been a half-step — a greeting, a turn into playfulness, then a near-silence. There’s movement toward something, but the door hasn’t opened yet.

What’s underneath

Something here isn’t ready to be named — and that’s alright.

The energy arrived quietly, then went quieter still: neutral shading toward unpleasant, neither grasping nor pushing. It’s the feeling of sitting at the edge of the water, unsure whether to wade in.

The practice

1.

Sit at the edge

Don’t try to name what’s going on yet. Just notice: is there something here, even without words? Let that be enough.

2.

Follow the next honest syllable

When something does stir — a feeling, an image, even a ‘sort of’ — say that small thing out loud, however incomplete it seems.

3.

Notice what brought you here

Something drew you to this door today, even if it was just restlessness. Sit with that one thing — not to solve it, just to look at it gently.
