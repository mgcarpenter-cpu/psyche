How payouts work

You earn $10 a month for every monthly paying member, and $100 a year for every annual paying member, who joined through your link, for as long as they stay. Earning starts when you are a paying member yourself. New earnings are held for 30 days while the payment clears, then they are ready. Ready earnings of $50 or more are paid to your account on the 1st of each month. If a payment behind your commission is refunded or reversed, that commission is removed. You're responsible for any tax on your earnings — your statements above are your record, ready for your accountant.
