How payouts work
▾
Commission is held for 30 days while the member’s payment clears, then paid on the 1st of each month once your balance is over $50.
A refund or chargeback inside the 30 days removes that commission before it’s ever paid. If one lands after payout, the amount is clawed back from your next balance.
Pending amounts aren’t final until they clear.
