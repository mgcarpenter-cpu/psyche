How payouts work

You earn 30% of every payment, excluding tax, from members who joined through your link — for as long as they stay. New commission stays pending for up to 12 days while the payment settles, then it's ready. Ready earnings of $50 or more are paid to your account on the 1st and 15th of each month. If a payment behind your commission is refunded or reversed, that commission is removed. You're responsible for any tax on your earnings — your statements above are your record, ready for your accountant.
