Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Psyche (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides an AI-based reflection and coaching tool at psyche.ai. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are based in New Zealand and handle personal information in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, and — where it applies — the EU/UK GDPR.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address, and basic details from Google if you sign in that way.
  • Your conversations — the messages you send to Psyche and the responses generated, so we can provide the service and maintain continuity across your sessions.
  • Usage information — things like how many messages you’ve sent, your plan, and basic technical data (device type, browser, approximate region) needed to run and secure the service.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by our payment provider (Paddle). We do not see or store your full card details.

Sensitive information

Conversations with Psyche may contain personal and sensitive information about your thoughts, feelings, and circumstances. We treat this content with care and only use it to provide the service to you. We do not sell it, and we do not use it to identify or profile you for advertising.

We do not use your conversations to train AI models. Your messages are sent to our AI provider only to generate your reply in the moment — not to train or improve any model.

How we use your information

  • To provide Psyche and generate responses to you.
  • To remember context across your sessions, so conversations feel continuous.
  • To manage your account, plan, and message limits.
  • To improve the service, understand how it’s used, and fix problems.
  • To communicate with you about your account or important changes.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run Psyche. They process data only on our instructions and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Anthropic — the AI provider that generates Psyche’s responses. Your messages are sent to Anthropic’s API to produce a reply.
  • Supabase — secure storage for your account and conversation data.
  • Paddle — payment processing and subscription billing.
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside New Zealand (including in the United States and other countries). Where we transfer your information overseas, we take reasonable steps to ensure it remains protected.

Data retention

We keep your account and conversation data while your account is active, so your conversations stay available to you. If you delete your account, we delete your associated personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to keep limited records (for example, for tax or legal reasons).

Your rights and choices

  • You can access and review your conversations within the app.
  • You can delete your account at any time, which removes your associated data.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your personal information, or ask us to correct it.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy law — for example, the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA. These rights apply based on where you are, and we’ll honour valid requests under them.

To make any of these requests, email support@psyche.ai.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No online service can be perfectly secure, but we take protecting your data seriously.

Age

Psyche is intended for people aged 18 and over. It is not designed for, or directed at, children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we’ll let you know through the service or by email. The “last updated” date above shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at support@psyche.ai.
