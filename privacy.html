Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Sussuri is a reflection and coaching tool built around private conversation. Privacy is the reason it can work at all. This page explains plainly what we collect, why, who helps us run it, and what we never do.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and your name if you give it. Sign-in is by email link or Google. We never see or store a password.
  • Your conversations: what you type or say to Sussuri and its replies, stored so your coaching can carry on from where you left off.
  • Know yourself answers and your goals: stored so Sussuri can be personal to you.
  • Practice & schedule: what you chose to be reminded about, your times and your time zone, so reminders arrive when you asked for them.
  • Personal meditations: what you asked for, the script written for you and the finished recording, kept so you can listen again.
  • Membership and usage: which plan you are on, and how much of your monthly allowance you have used.
  • Affiliate information: if you share Sussuri, we record who joined from your link and whether they became a paying member, so you can be paid. Where you are paid is held by our payment provider.
  • Share cards: the words you type on a share card. A share card is public: anyone with its link can read it.
  • Anonymous website visits: the page, the site or link that sent you, your country and city, and whether you are on a phone, tablet or computer. A small cookie holds a random ID so repeat visits count once. We don't store your IP address, and this isn't linked to your name.

How your conversations are processed

Your messages are sent to our AI providers to produce a reply. Text is processed by Anthropic. Voice is processed by Google (Gemini). We only use them under terms where your conversations are not used to train their models.

Your conversations are never sold and never shared with advertisers. A person at Sussuri does not read them, with three exceptions: you send one to us in a support or feedback request, the law requires it, or we reasonably believe it is needed to prevent serious harm to you or someone else.

Voice

When you speak to Sussuri, your speech is processed in real time to produce a transcript and a spoken reply. The audio is not stored. Only the text is kept, as part of your conversation.

Personal meditations

When you ask for a personal meditation, what you tell Sussuri is used to write a script for you. The script is sent to ElevenLabs, which turns it into the spoken recording. The recording is stored in your account and is only available to you.

Reminders

Reminders are sent as Sussuri notifications and by email, only for the things you ticked on Practice & schedule. Untick them there and they stop.

Where data lives

Your information is stored with Supabase using industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. These companies hold and process data outside New Zealand, mainly in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, your conversations, answers, goals and personal meditations are deleted. Records of payments and commission are kept for as long as tax law requires.

What we never do

  • No selling of personal data, to anyone, ever.
  • No advertising trackers in the app.
  • No AI training on your conversations by our AI providers.
  • No reading your conversations, except as described above.

How Sussuri improves

We look at patterns in how Sussuri performs, such as where conversations help and where they fall short, to improve our own coaching. Where practical we use combined or de-identified information for this. This work stays inside Sussuri and is never shared or sold.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: use Delete account on the Account & earnings page, or email hello@sussuri.com. Deletion is permanent.
  • A copy of your data: email hello@sussuri.com and we will send it.
  • Reminders: switch them on or off on Practice & schedule.
  • Ambience & preferences: stored on your device, not our servers.

Sussuri is based in New Zealand and handles your information under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, and you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner. Where you are in another region, additional laws (such as the GDPR) may give you further rights.

Important limits

Sussuri is a reflection tool, not therapy or crisis care. If a conversation suggests you may be in crisis, we show support options. We do not contact anyone on your behalf.

Children

Sussuri is for adults. It is not directed at, and must not be used by, anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll note it here with a new date, and for significant changes we'll tell you in the app.
