Payment terms

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This page explains how payments work at Sussuri: what you pay us, and what we pay you if you take part in the affiliate program.

Paying for Sussuri

Your first month is free. A card is needed to start it and is not charged during the free month. After that, membership is $39 a month or $349 a year, in US dollars, and renews until you cancel. Any tax that applies is added and shown before you confirm.

Payments are handled by our payment provider, which sells the membership to you and sends your receipts. We never see or store your card details.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time on the Membership page. If you cancel during the free month, you are not charged. If you cancel later, you keep your membership until the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not charged again.

Refunds

You have a full free month to try Sussuri, and we email you a reminder three days before your first charge. For that reason, payments are not refunded once they have been charged, except where consumer law gives you that right (including the New Zealand Consumer Guarantees Act where it applies). If you believe you were charged in error, email hello@sussuri.com and we will put it right.

Affiliate program: how you earn

Paid members can share Sussuri with their own link. You earn $10 a month for each paying member who joined through your link on the monthly plan, or $100 a year for each on the annual plan, for as long as they stay a paying member. Nothing is earned during a friend's free month. Earning starts with their first payment and stops when their membership ends.

You earn only while you are a paying member yourself. Referring yourself, spam, misleading promotion and artificial sign-ups cancel earnings. We make no promise about how much, if anything, you will earn, and we only ever show amounts actually earned or paid, never projected figures.

Holding period

Because card payments can be disputed or refunded for some time after they're made, commission is held before it becomes payable. Each commission clears 30 days after it is earned. This protects the program: we only pay on payments that have settled.

Payouts

Cleared commission is paid on the 1st of each month, provided your available balance is over $50. Balances under $50 roll over to the following month. Depending on when commission clears, your first payment may take up to two months to arrive.

Any transfer fee is deducted from the payout and shown to you. You must give a valid place to be paid before any payout is released, and you're responsible for any tax on your earnings.

Refunds and clawback

If a payment on which commission was already paid is later refunded or charged back, that commission is reversed and deducted from your next balance (a clawback). You agree to this as a condition of taking part; if your balance is not enough, we may recover it from future earnings.

Closing your account

If you close your account, any cleared, available balance is paid to your registered details. Commission still within the holding period has not cleared and is forfeited. If you'd rather keep it, wait until pending amounts are paid before closing.

Changes

Amounts, the holding period, the payout minimum and payout dates are described in the app and may be updated with reasonable notice. We may change, suspend, or end the affiliate program at any time; commission already cleared under these terms will still be paid.
