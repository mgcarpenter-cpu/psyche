What Psyche is — and isn't

Psyche is an AI-powered reflection and coaching tool grounded in contemplative psychology. It is not therapy, medical care, or crisis support, and it is not a substitute for any of them. AI can be wrong; treat Psyche's words as a mirror to think with, not instructions to follow. If you are in crisis, use the support options in the app or contact local emergency services.

Referrals and earnings

If you share Psyche with your link, you may earn commission on subscriptions from people who join through it, for as long as their subscription remains active. Commission is a percentage of the subscription, set by your membership tier, and is paid only on active, paying subscribers. Commission stops when a referred subscription ends. Self-referrals, spam, misleading promotion, or artificial sign-ups void earnings.

Because card payments can be disputed or refunded for some time after they are made, commission is held before it becomes payable. Each commission clears 30 days after it is earned. This holding period protects the program: it means we only pay on revenue that has settled and is unlikely to be reversed.

Cleared commission is paid once a month, on a single set payout date, provided your available balance is over $50. Balances under $50 roll over to the following month. Depending on when commission clears relative to the monthly payout date, your first payment may take up to two months to arrive. A transfer fee is deducted from each payout and shown before you confirm.

If a payment on which commission was already paid is later refunded or charged back, that commission is reversed and deducted from your next balance (a clawback). If you close your account, any cleared, available balance is paid to your registered details; commission still within the holding period has not cleared and is forfeited.

Rates, holding period, thresholds and payout dates are described in the app and may be updated with reasonable notice.
